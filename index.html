Privacy Policy — QUIT

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QUIT — Stop Smoking ("QUIT", "we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our smoking cessation application.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, QUIT may process account and authentication identifiers, your quit plan and preferences, smoking cessation progress, craving and trigger records, and messages you submit to the AI Coach.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in AI Coach messages unless it is necessary for your request.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • authenticate users and provide app features;
  • save or calculate quit plans and progress;
  • respond to messages sent to the AI Coach;
  • enforce usage limits and protect the service from abuse;
  • maintain, secure, and improve the app.

3. Service Providers

QUIT uses third-party services to support its operation. These may include Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloudflare services for server-side request processing and usage records, and Google Gemini to generate AI Coach responses.

Information needed to provide a feature may be processed by these providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Their processing practices are governed by their own documentation.

4. AI Coach

If you use the AI Coach, the messages you submit and relevant conversation context may be sent to our server and to the AI provider to generate a response. AI responses may be inaccurate and are for general support only. QUIT is not a medical service and does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

5. Storage and Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service processing it.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

For questions about your information or to request access, correction, or deletion, contact us at the email below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

QUIT is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
